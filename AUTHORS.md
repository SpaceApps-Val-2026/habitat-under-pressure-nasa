# Team

Habitat Under Pressure was built by a team of five in València for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026:
[team page on spaceappschallenge.org](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/under-pressure1/?tab=members).

| Person | Main work |
| --- | --- |
| Iban Borràs Serret | Product and architecture; coordination; the web app and its design system; the engine (assistant and executor); presentation |
| Sara Fernández Ouanit | Data sources and the source catalogue; licences and attributions; pre-download and readers of the sources; legal norms and their quotes |
| Nadezhda Kuznetsova | Ecological relationships; evidence chains and their references; resource-use coefficients |
| Guillem Reverter Falcó | Statistical methods: trends, scenarios and uncertainty; analysis operations; the openings of the reports; deployment on Google Cloud Run |
| Lara Ruiz Casado | Team owner. Review of the exchange contracts and test questions for the assistant; reviewer rules; fixes in the API and the web app; QA suites |

## AI assistance

The code and the documents were written with AI coding agents (Claude, by Anthropic, and Codex, by OpenAI) working
under the direction of each team member, who reviewed and decided. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) describes how the agents
were asked to work. The language model inside the product writes no figures: every number comes from a source and a
saved calculation.
